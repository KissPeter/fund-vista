Traffic runway-funnel corridor collapse, backend/traffic/corridors.py (renderer r8, 2026-11-26).

Problem: one <path> per movement re-inks the same final-approach / climb-out line dozens of times on the runways and tears the paper. The plot pipeline's `linemerge` cannot help — it joins touching endpoints, not strokes drawn on top of each other. The fix must live in the traffic renderer so preview and plot share one render path.

Algorithm (`corridor_paths`): per movement layer, a stroke joins the closest corridor whose rolling union of the last `CORRIDOR_ANCHORS=4` accepted geometries covers `CORRIDOR_COVERAGE=0.85` of its samples (tolerance `CORRIDOR_TOL_FRAC=0.002` of canvas width, ~2 units / 0.6 mm). Union tracking — not the old seed-only comparison — keeps a slowly drifting funnel in one corridor. A covered retrace with a diverging tail emits only the uncovered tail as a short extra pass (max 2 tails per cluster), never a second full stroke. Sub-pen stubs (< 0.5 * tol length) are kept untouched. Samples are capped at `_SAMPLE_MAX=64`, and the rolling anchors are stacked into one segment array so each candidate costs one vectorised distance pass instead of four — the 4000-stroke perf test stayed inside budget after this change. Note: the local host can be heavily loaded (VM + ffmpeg), in which case `perf_budget` needs its documented `PERF_BUDGET_SCALE` override.

Density: `CORRIDOR_MAX_PASSES=10` hard caps full passes per corridor; `CORRIDOR_REFERENCE_STROKES=200` gives one pass per 20 movements (50-flight funnel -> 3 passes, 200+ -> 10). Request knob `RenderRequest.max_corridor_passes` (1..50, default 10) threads through `router` -> `render_svg`; it is in `_filter_fingerprint` and bumps `RENDER_VERSION` to r8 so cached r7 SVGs expire. Movement counts stay unchanged — a collapsed layer still reports its real traffic count.

Calibration: synthetic 200-flight identical funnel measured 23 full passes with the old seed-only tol=2/split=8; union + cap=10 now draws exactly 10 at any traffic volume. Raising tolerance alone (tol=4/split=20) reached 10 but over-merged distinct parallel corridors, so prefer the cap over a fatter tolerance.
